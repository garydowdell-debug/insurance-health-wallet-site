Everyone who has helped a parent knows the folder.

Insurance cards, pill bottles, appointment slips, and a stack of bills nobody can read. The information all exists. It is just never in one place, and never in plain language at the moment you need it — standing at a pharmacy counter, or in a waiting room filling in the same form for the fourth time.

This app is the folder, carried in your pocket and able to explain itself.

“Which card is the current one?”
“Did Mom take the blue pill today?”
“What did the doctor say to do next?”
“Is this bill for something that happened?”

What it does

Six things, each aimed at a moment where the paperwork usually wins.

🪪

Reads your insurance card

Photograph a card or a benefits booklet and it pulls out the copays, deductibles and coverage limits — then explains what they mean.

💊

Medicine reminders that follow up

A reminder before the dose, at the dose, and again afterwards if it still is not marked as taken. Medicines you take only when needed stay quiet.

🗓️

Appointments, and the questions you meant to ask

What is coming up, what you wanted to ask, and what the doctor asked you to do next — so it does not get lost on the drive home.

📖

Checks your medical bills

Add an Explanation of Benefits and it compares every charge against the visits you logged. A bill for something that never happened stands out.

🚑

One printable page for an emergency

Medicines, allergies, conditions, insurance and who to call — on one sheet, for hospital staff or whoever is helping that day.

🏠

Built for a whole family

Track a spouse, a parent, a child — or someone you simply help out. Share one wallet so nobody is guessing about anyone else’s care.

About your privacy

This app holds medical information, which is about as private as information gets. So, plainly.

Read the full privacy policy

Your information is yours. Nobody else sees it unless you invite them.

Nothing is sold, rented, or used for advertising. There are no ads and no tracking.

You can delete everything, permanently, whenever you want.

When you ask it to read a document, that document is sent to an AI service to be read. Nothing is sent unless you ask.
